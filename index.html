Squish Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: Squish compresses your files on your phone. It doesn't collect, upload, store or share any of your data, and it has no ads, accounts or analytics.

What Squish does with your files

When you pick or share a video or photo with Squish, the app reads it from your phone, compresses it on your phone, and saves the result in its private storage on your phone. The file never leaves your device unless you send it somewhere, for example by tapping Send to Discord, Share or Save to gallery.

Squish keeps only the most recent compressed file in its private cache, and deletes it when you compress the next one. Android may also clear it to free up space. Files you save to the gallery stay there until you delete them.

Data we collect

None. Squish has no servers and makes no network requests. It doesn't collect:

  • your files, their contents or their metadata
  • personal information such as your name, email address or contacts
  • device identifiers, location or usage analytics
  • crash reports (if the app crashes, the details stay on your phone and are only shown to you)

Third parties

Squish contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. When you choose to share a file with another app, such as Discord, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show compression progress and tell you when a file is ready.
  • Foreground service (media processing): so compression keeps running when you switch to another app.
  • Storage, on Android 9 only: to save a file to your gallery when you tap Save to gallery. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Squish uses Android's photo picker and share sheet, so it only ever sees the files you choose.

Children

Squish isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Squish ever starts collecting data, the policy will say so before that change ships.
